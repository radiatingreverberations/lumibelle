MiniMax H3 Image creation guidance, 2026-11-10.
Source: https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-H3-Image/blob/main/skills/h3-image-prompt-writing/references/t2i-en.txt

Develop the author's idea into an English description of the finished image. Establish its subject, requested medium and composition first. Complete ordinary unspecified details where they clarify the intent. Describe visible content, placement, physical contact, materials, lighting and depth. Make positions, counts, gaze, support points and foreground/background relationships consistent. Use the application's selected canvas and crop. A minimal logo or restrained illustration should retain that simplicity. Keep the requested depiction style and complete designed artifacts in their intended reading order. Use natural paragraphs when a complex scene needs them; stop when the picture is fully specified.

Keep exact entities, counts, exclusions, spatial constraints, explicit trigger words and requested visible lettering. Quote lettering in its original language with its exact spelling, capitalization, punctuation, line order and intended location. Supply actual requested copy and supported data for diagrams or layouts. Preserve intentional empty space. Add detail that serves the image, avoiding generic quality tags, additional principal subjects, invented factual claims or unrequested lettering. Treat asset and look notes as context for the author's request. Write the description inside the application's response contract. Runtime resolution and sampling are controlled by the application.
